Finance Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short: everything you enter stays on your device. Finance Tracker has no account, no servers, no analytics and no tracking. I never see your data.

Your data stays on your device

Accounts, categories, budgets and transactions are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. The app does not send them anywhere and makes no network requests of its own. There is no sign-up and no account.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Face ID and passcode (App Lock)

If you turn on App Lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode. The app only receives a yes or no answer. It never sees or stores biometric data.

Widgets

To show your widgets, the app saves a small summary (such as this period's totals and budget progress) to a shared container on your device that only Finance Tracker and its widgets can read. This summary never leaves your device. When App Lock is on, widgets hide amounts.

Exporting your data

CSV files are created only when you tap Export CSV backup. You choose where they go using the iOS share sheet. The app deletes its temporary copies afterwards. Once you share the files, the privacy policy of wherever you send them applies.

Purchases

If the app offers optional purchases, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. The app only learns whether a purchase is active. I never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID.

Device backups

Like other apps, Finance Tracker's data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, depending on your device settings. These backups are handled by Apple and are not accessible to me.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device, including the widget summary.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds iCloud sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released to explain what is synced and how. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
