How do I turn on App Lock?

Go to Settings and turn on Require Face ID or passcode. Your iPhone needs a passcode set. If you later remove the device passcode, App Lock turns itself off and the app tells you. If Face ID doesn't recognise you, you can use your passcode instead.

My widgets aren't updating

  • Open the app once. Widgets refresh whenever you add or change something.
  • iOS limits how often widgets refresh, so updates can sometimes be delayed by a few minutes.
  • If a widget still looks stuck, remove it and add it again.
  • Amounts showing as hidden? That's expected when App Lock is on, to keep them private on your Home Screen.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings → Export CSV backup. The app creates five CSV files (accounts, categories, expenses, incomes and transfers) and opens the share sheet so you can save them to Files, AirDrop them or send them elsewhere. They open in any spreadsheet app.

How do I delete all my data?

Your data lives only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes everything: touch and hold the Finance Tracker icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Export a CSV backup first if you want to keep a copy. Older iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone may still contain the app's data until those backups are replaced.
